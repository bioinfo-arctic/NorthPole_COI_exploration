Methods & data sources

Coordinates, sample names, dates and depths come from curated_metadata_by_sample.txt. Composition comes from the existing diversity_analysis/surface_composition results.

  • Previously applied taxonomic exclusions and blank-dominance cleanup are preserved.
  • Surface libraries with at least 5,000 cleaned reads are retained, then technical replicates are summed by curated sample name.
  • Percentages include all pooled reads. These are sequence-read proportions, not organism abundances.
  • Unclassified includes unresolved labels at the selected rank, including Correct_manually. Other combines named taxa below 1% in every pooled surface sample.
  • S01 is mapped but has no composition because all its libraries were below 5,000 cleaned reads.

Coastline: Natural Earth public-domain data. All geometry and sample data are embedded. No network connection is needed.
